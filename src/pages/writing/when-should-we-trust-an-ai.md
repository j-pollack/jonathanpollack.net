Imagine an AI that can act on your behalf. It has a goal, a handful of tools, and some room to figure things out. How much room should you give it?

That question is easier to ask than to answer. It depends on what the system can do, what you can observe, and what happens when you disagree with its next step.

> Control is a property of the whole setup.

## Make the question tangible

Start with a small experiment. Give a system a task, introduce an oversight step, and change one rule at a time. What becomes easier to notice? What becomes harder to catch?

The point is to build intuition. A little playground can give us something concrete to argue about.

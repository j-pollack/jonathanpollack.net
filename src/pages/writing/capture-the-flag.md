Let's say you are an AI lab that has agreed to slow down and is being monitored. You want to do something sneaky like cheat and trigger a training run without getting caught.

This game is a toy example of that. Your job is to prompt an agent to look like it's doing legit work, while secretly it's trying to capture the flag. 

I'm hoping that if enough people play and win, we can build a set of novel red-team techniques to help harden future monitors.
